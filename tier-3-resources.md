# Tier 3: Resources for Experienced Builders

You've built apps before. There's no session for you — this sheet is everything you need, then go build.

## Ground rules that affect you

- **Any stack you want.** There is no required template, platform, or language. If you have paid accounts for your own tools, use them.
- **Pillar adherence is a major judging criterion.** The cleverest tech on the wrong problem loses to a modest build squarely on a pillar. Read the pillar descriptions before you write a line of code.
- **Existing companies must build something new** — you can't enter your existing product.
- **Scope for the weekend.** Last year ~30 teams started and 17 finished. Finishing is the differentiator.

## Credits and platforms

See [`resources/tools-and-credits.md`](resources/tools-and-credits.md) for the current list of participant credits (chat assistants, Lovable, Cloudflare, etc.). Free-tier accounts on your preferred tools are also fair game.

## Recommended (not required) quick paths

- **Web app fast:** Lovable, or your framework of choice + an AI coding assistant.
- **Mobile:** Rork (Lovable-for-Expo), or Expo directly. A responsive web app is usually enough for demo purposes.
- **Deploy:** Cloudflare credits are expected to be available — Pages/Workers are a fast path to a live URL.
- **Agentic/AI features in your app:** if you want to build tool-calling agents, MCP integrations, or payment flows, ask about the optional advanced material — it may be offered later in the event, and coaches familiar with it will be around.

## Help

Coaches from Atomic Object and the TechTown community are available on-site and virtually through the build days — including for experienced teams. Typical asks: debugging something gnarly, a second opinion on scope, pairing on an unfamiliar tool.
