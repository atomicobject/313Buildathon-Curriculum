# 2026 Venture 313 AI Buildathon — Technical Curriculum

Curriculum and getting-started material for the third annual Venture 313 AI Buildathon, hosted by ID Ventures / TechTown Detroit.

**Event dates:** September 17–22, 2026, Detroit

| Date | What happens |
|------|-------------|
| Thu Sept 17 | Kickoff + team formation |
| Fri Sept 18 | Technical curriculum sessions (this repo) |
| Sat–Sun Sept 19–20 | Build days, with on-site and virtual support |
| Mon Sept 21 | Finalist selection |
| Tue Sept 22 | Showcase |

Teams build solutions to Detroit-specific problems drawn from Mayor Sheffield's citizen survey pillars. Adherence to your chosen pillar is a major judging criterion.

## How the curriculum works

There is **no required course, template, or tech stack**. Use whatever tools you know and love. The purpose of this material is to lower the floor: to make sure that people who aren't already comfortable coding have everything they need to participate and finish something they're proud of.

On Friday Sept 18 we'll run **short, scheduled sessions**, about 30 minutes of content plus Q&A, offered at multiple times (exact schedule TBD). Pick the one that matches your experience:

1. **[I've never used an AI agent](sessions/tier-1-intro-to-ai-agents.md)**: a crash course on working with an AI assistant. You'll leave able to develop your idea in conversation and produce something real — a concept, a pitch, a mockup — without writing code.
2. **[I've chatted with AI, but never built an app](sessions/tier-2-build-with-ai-tools.md)**: a directed workflow: shape your idea with a chat assistant, then hand it to a build tool to make a working app.
3. **[I've built apps before](tier-3-resources.md)**: no session needed. Here's a resource sheet with the available credits, recommended tools, and where to find help. Knock yourself out.

Not sure which fits? Start one tier lower than you think — you can always leave early.

## Getting help

Throughout the build days there will be technical coaches on hand (on-site and virtual). Most teams won't need much help; if you're stuck, that's exactly what the coaches are for. Don't burn an hour on a problem a coach can help you resolve in five minutes.
